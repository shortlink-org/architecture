## Context

The Google Analytics snippet lived in the codebase, which meant slower page loads, a developer involved in every
tracking change, and no way to adjust tracking quickly.

## Decision

Integrate Google Analytics through Cloudflare Apps, detaching the script from the codebase and managing the setup in
Cloudflare's interface — with room for further integrations on the same platform.

## Consequences

**Gained** — tracking changes without a deploy, potentially faster page loads, developers freed from maintaining
analytics code.

**Cost** — a careful transition to avoid losing data, a new dependency on Cloudflare for analytics availability, and
a privacy review of how the data is handled.
