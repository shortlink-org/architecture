## Context

Tracing and logging only allow events to be compared across services if spans and attributes are named the same way
everywhere.

## Decision

Adopt the conventions in [Naming Spans and Attributes](https://uptrace.dev/opentelemetry/span-naming.html) rather
than defining a house standard.

## Consequences

A short ADR with a large reach: it is what makes a trace readable when it crosses from the TypeScript proxy into the
Go link service. The proxy's own `traceparent` propagation on published events depends on both ends agreeing on
naming, not just on protocol.
