## Context

The platform needs a standard way to document its architecture. Models evaluated: [C4](https://c4model.com/), 4+1,
ARC42, ArchiMate, diagrams.net. Tools evaluated: Archi (including archi → neo4j → grafana), PlantUML, C4-PlantUML,
OpenAPI, Gherkin, Miro, Notion, [diagrams](https://diagrams.mingrammer.com/).

## Decision

- The **C4 model** for describing architecture
- **PlantUML** with [C4-PlantUML](https://github.com/plantuml-stdlib/C4-PlantUML) for drawing it

## Consequences

This is why nearly every service README in the monorepo opens with a `plantuml` block including
`C4_Container.puml`, and why boundaries carry a `0003-c4-system` ADR of their own.

It is also the decision this catalog partly supersedes in practice: EventCatalog generates context and resource
diagrams from frontmatter rather than from hand-drawn PlantUML. The two coexist — the C4 diagrams in the source
repositories remain the authored view, the catalog's are derived.

The drawing half of this decision has since been amended. C4 is still the model, but inside this catalog the diagrams
are written in LikeC4 rather than C4-PlantUML — see
[ADR-0043](../adr-platform-0043-likec4-for-c4-diagrams). C4-PlantUML remains in the source repositories.
