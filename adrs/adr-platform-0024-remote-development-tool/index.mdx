## Context

Developing, debugging and deploying from a local machine to a remote Kubernetes environment needs tool support.
Candidates: [Skaffold](https://skaffold.dev/), [Devfile.io](https://devfile.io/),
[GitHub Codespaces](https://github.com/features/codespaces).

## Decision

**Skaffold** — it supports rapid local Kubernetes development, is lightweight, and covers the whole development
lifecycle.

## Consequences

The team needs training on it, and its resource usage needs watching. The ADR explicitly leaves the door open:
the alternatives should be reassessed if the need arises.

A `skaffold.yaml` at the monorepo root is the artefact of this decision.
