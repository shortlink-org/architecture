## Context

Operational decisions need consistent, comparable data across services.

## Decision

**SLA / SLO / SLI**

- `service_availability_ratio` — gauge, uptime over total time
- `http_request_duration_seconds` — histogram, response times
- `error_rate_per_minute` — counter

**Cache**

- `cache_hit_total`, `cache_miss_total` — counters
- `cache_hit_ratio` — derived from the two above
- `hot_key_access_frequency` — gauge

**Load** — `requests_per_second`, `transactions_per_second`, `queries_per_second`, `response_time_seconds`,
`error_rate_percentage`

**Resources** — `network_traffic_bytes`, `cpu_usage_percentage`, `ram_usage_bytes`, `disk_usage_bytes`

**Other** — `queue_size`, `process_count`, `thread_count`

Names follow [[adr|adr-platform-0023-naming-prometheus-metrics]].

## Consequences

The cache metrics are the ones with teeth in this catalog: they are what makes the hit ratio on the
[[container|proxy-redis-cache]] an observable number rather than an assumption — which matters, because the proxy
bypasses that cache entirely for authenticated requests.

The ADR notes its own risk: distributed systems make some of these hard to measure accurately, and quantitative
metrics can be over-trusted.
