## Context

The platform spans many services and libraries. Reference: [Understanding Monorepos](https://monorepo.tools/#understanding-monorepos).

## Decision

Use a monorepository.

## Consequences

Services are grouped by bounded context under `boundaries/`, which is the structure this catalog's domains mirror.

The decision is not absolute in practice: the [[domain|Auth]] boundary lives in its own repository
(`shortlink-org/auth`), and this catalog is a third. The monorepo holds the platform's core, not everything —
which is why the schema sync in this repository has to fetch from more than one source.

[[adr|adr-platform-0036-nx-monorepo]] covers the tooling attempt on top of this decision, and its rollback.
