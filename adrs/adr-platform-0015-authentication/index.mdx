## Context

The platform needs to decide how users are authenticated. The options surveyed were OIDC over OAuth 2.0
(RFC 6749, 6750, 8414, 8705, 8693), JWT (RFC 7515, 7516, 7519, 7523), and 2FA.

## Decision

Use third-party authentication rather than building it — it is more secure, and it is not a service the team wants
to operate itself.

Candidates researched: [Keycloak](https://www.keycloak.org/), [ory/hydra](https://www.ory.sh/hydra/),
[dexidp/dex](https://dexidp.io/).

The ADR ends with a plan rather than a choice: *"Step 1. Try to use Keycloak — install it, try it, make a decision
and update this ADR."*

## Consequences

> [!NOTE]
> The ADR was never updated with the outcome. What the platform actually runs is **Ory Kratos** for authentication
> and **SpiceDB** for authorization — see [[domain|Auth]] and
> [[adr|adr-auth-0001-zero-trust-authentication-flow]]. Keycloak is not part of the request path.
>
> The principle in this ADR held; the specific next step recorded in it did not become the answer.
