## Context

Architectural decisions made on the project need to be recorded.

## Decision

Use Architecture Decision Records in the format [described by Michael Nygard](http://thinkrelevance.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions),
with [adr-tools](https://github.com/npryce/adr-tools) as a lightweight toolset.

## Consequences

Every decision in this catalog's ADR collection traces back to this one. The format — Context, Decision,
Consequences, and a Status — is why each boundary carries its own `docs/ADR/decisions` directory, and why this
catalog can mirror them one-to-one.

The ADR itself records a reservation: an [RFC process](https://philcalcado.com/2018/11/19/a_structured_rfc_process.html)
is noted as worth considering as an alternative.
