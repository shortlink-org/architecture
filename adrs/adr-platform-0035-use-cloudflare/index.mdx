## Context

Growing traffic brought security, performance and scalability pressure: protection against DDoS, content delivery
optimisation, and traffic management.

## Decision

Route all incoming and outgoing traffic through Cloudflare — CDN, DDoS protection, WAF and SSL — in front of the
Istio/Envoy edge and the Kubernetes cluster.

**Drop existing Envoy response headers** so internal architecture details are not exposed, replacing them with
Cloudflare's own.

## Consequences

**Gained** — a smaller attack surface, lower latency worldwide, centralised traffic management with real-time
insight, and less load on origin servers.

**Cost** — a hard dependency on a third party for critical infrastructure, premium service costs, and privacy
considerations from the Google Analytics integration that comes with it.

For this catalog it means the public entry point sits one layer above anything documented here: the
[[system|access-proxy-system]] is the *cluster* edge, not the internet edge.
