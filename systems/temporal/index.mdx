## Overview

Temporal runs the long-lived processes of the shop: a cart that has to survive between sessions, an order that
moves through states over hours or days, and the delivery saga that spans two domains.

It is shared infrastructure — [[service|OMSService]] and [[service|DeliveryService]] both depend on it — which is
why it is documented once here rather than as a container inside either domain.

## Deployment

| | |
|---|---|
| Frontend | `temporal-frontend.temporal.svc.cluster.local:7233` |
| TLS | disabled in-cluster (`TEMPORAL_TLS_ENABLED: "false"`) |

Namespaces are separated per domain, so a workflow in one boundary cannot see or signal a workflow in the other:

| Namespace | Owner | Task queues |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| `oms` | [[service|OMSService]] | cart and order workflows |
| `delivery` | [[service|DeliveryService]] | `DELIVERY_TASK_QUEUE`, `COURIER_TASK_QUEUE` |

## Why it is here

[[adr|adr-oms-0003-temporal]] chose it over a hand-rolled state machine, Kafka event sourcing and database polling,
for durable execution, built-in retries and workflow visibility. [[adr|adr-oms-0004-hexagonal-architecture-with-temporal]]
then constrained how it is allowed to reach into the domain.

> [!NOTE]
> Temporal is durable execution, not a message bus. Facts that leave a boundary still go through Kafka via the
> outbox — see [[adr|adr-oms-0005-reliable-domain-events-outbox]].
