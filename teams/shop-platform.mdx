## Overview

The Shop Platform team owns two boundaries that ship from one repository: [[domain|Shop]] (goods, cart, order,
pricing, storefront) and [[domain|Delivery]] (packages and couriers).

They are separate domains because the aggregates, the databases and the Temporal namespaces are separate — but one
team carries both, so a change that crosses the boundary does not cross an organisation.

## Responsibilities

- **Goods catalogue** — the Django back office and the reference data behind it
- **Cart and order lifecycle** — the OMS aggregates and their Temporal workflows
- **Pricing** — tax and discount policy, expressed in Rego
- **Federated graph** — the Cosmo Router and the subgraphs behind it
- **Delivery** — package state machine, courier dispatch and tracking

## Repository

All services live in [shortlink-org/shop](https://github.com/shortlink-org/shop) as top-level directories.
