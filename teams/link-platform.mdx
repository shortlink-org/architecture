## Overview

The Link Platform team owns the [[domain|Link]] boundary: creating and managing short links, serving redirects, and
enriching links with metadata from their target pages.

## Responsibilities

- **Link aggregate** — the domain model, hashing strategy and storage
- **Public API** — the REST BFF and the gRPC contracts behind it
- **Redirect path** — the proxy, its cache and its latency budget
- **Enrichment** — metadata extraction and the CQRS read model it feeds

## Repository

All services live in the monorepo [shortlink-org/shortlink](https://github.com/shortlink-org/shortlink) under
`boundaries/`: `link`, `bff`, `proxy`, `metadata`.
