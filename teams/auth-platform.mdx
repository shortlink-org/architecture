## Overview

The Auth Platform team owns the [[domain|Auth]] boundary: everything that answers "who is this request from?" and "is this request allowed?".

## Responsibilities

- **Identity** — Ory Kratos deployment, identity schema, self-service flows (login, registration, recovery, verification, settings)
- **Edge authentication** — Ory Oathkeeper access rules, JWT minting for internal services
- **Permissions** — the SpiceDB schema and the auth service that migrates it
- **Auth UI** — the Next.js application rendering Kratos self-service flows
