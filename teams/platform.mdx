## Overview

The Platform team owns the decisions that are not any single domain's to make: how services expose health and
metrics, how events are named, how secrets are handled, how databases are provisioned, and which tools the codebase
standardises on.

Those decisions live as ADRs at the root of this catalog rather than inside a domain, and are referenced from the
domains they constrain.
