## Overview

Produced by [[service|MetadataService]], consumed by [[service|LinkService]] to update the read model. The
`metadata` prefix marks it as owned by a different service than the rest of the domain's topics.

<NodeGraph />

## Provisioning

Declared as a Strimzi `KafkaTopic` in the `kafka` namespace by the chart of the service that publishes it:

| | |
|---|---|
| Partitions | 1 |
| Replicas | 3 |
| Retention | **2 hours** (`retention.ms: 7200000`) |
| Segment size | 30MB |

> [!WARNING]
> Two hours of retention is short for an event log. A consumer that is down longer than that does not catch up — it
> skips whatever expired. Replaying history is not available on this channel.
