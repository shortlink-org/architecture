## Overview

What `LinkQueryService.Get` and `List` return. It exists in [[container|link-cqrs-readmodel]] only — there is no
`LinkView` in the write store and no service that owns it as an aggregate.

## Two descriptions

`describe` is what the link's owner typed. `description` is what [[service|MetadataService]] scraped from the target
page. They coexist on the view and mean different things — a detail easy to get wrong when building a UI on top of
this.

## Freshness

Populated in two independent steps: the link fields when the link changes, the metadata fields when
[[event|MetadataExtracted]] arrives. Between those two moments the view is real but incomplete.
