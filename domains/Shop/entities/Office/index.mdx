## Overview

Reference data owned by [[service|AdminService]] for orders collected in person rather than delivered.

## Why this entity forces PostGIS

`location` is a `PointField` with SRID 4326, so [[container|shop-postgres]] needs the geospatial extension. It is
the only entity on the platform that does.

The alternative — two float columns — was not taken, which means proximity questions ("which office is nearest")
are answerable in the database rather than in application code.

## Not connected to delivery

An office is a place a customer comes to. It is unrelated to the pickup address on a [[entity|Package]], which is
carried on the delivery request and never resolved against this table.
