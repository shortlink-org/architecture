## The step that surprises people

`RequestDelivery` is a **synchronous gRPC call**, not a published event.
[[event|OrderDeliveryRequested]] is published alongside it and consumed by nobody — see
[[channel|oms.order.delivery_requested_event.v1]].

Durability comes from Temporal: the activity retries transient failures and refuses to retry validation, contract
and configuration errors.

## Where it continues

Everything after the package is created is [[flow|DeliverPackage]].
