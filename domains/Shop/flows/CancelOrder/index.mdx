## Two states, two very different cancellations

`PENDING` and `PROCESSING` both accept CANCEL, and the event looks the same from both. Whether delivery was already
requested is not on the event — a consumer has to look at whether the order carries a `package_id`.

## Cancelling stops the order, not the shipment

[[flow|Checkout]] hands the order to [[domain|Delivery]] with a synchronous [[command|AcceptOrder]], and
[[service|DeliveryService]] has no counterpart to it. Cancel after that point and the [[entity|Package]] keeps its
own state machine: it can still be assigned to a courier, carried and reported back — and
[[service|OMSService]] will apply those updates to an order that is already cancelled.

Nothing in the catalog compensates for this today. It is the clearest gap in the Shop ↔ Delivery contract.

## It never reaches the leaderboard

`COMPLETED` is the only state that feeds [[flow|GoodsLeaderboard]], so a cancelled order simply never counts.
