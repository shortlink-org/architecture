## What is this?

The Django database behind [[service|AdminService]] — the authoritative store for [[entity|Good]] and
[[entity|Office]]. Credentials come from the `shortlink-postgres-pguser-shop` secret.

## Why PostGIS

[[entity|Office]] stores its position as a GeoDjango `PointField` with SRID 4326 (WGS84), not as two float columns.
That makes the geospatial extension a hard requirement of this database, unlike every other Postgres instance on
the platform.

## History tables

Both models use `simple_history`, so every write also appends to a `historical_*` table. The catalogue therefore
carries its own audit trail without an event stream — which is part of why the catalogue publishes no events at
all.

## Not the order store

Orders and carts live in [[container|oms-postgres]] under a different credential and a different service. Nothing
joins across the two: the order engine holds a good's id, not a foreign key.
