## Overview

The reference-data half of the domain. [[service|AdminService]] is a Django application that owns
[[entity|Good]] and [[entity|Office]]; [[service|AdminUI]] is the back office over it; [[service|AdminGraphQL]] is a
typed adapter that lets the rest of the platform read goods through GraphQL instead of Django's REST API.

## Why Django

The job here is create, edit and list a catalogue with an admin UI over it — the one shape Django is unambiguously
good at. [[adr|adr-admin-0003-rest-framework]] covers the REST layer put in front of it for the public API.

The cost is that the goods catalogue speaks a different protocol from everything else in the domain, which is
exactly what [[service|AdminGraphQL]] exists to hide.

## Goods do not flow as events

Nothing in this system publishes to Kafka. Goods are read synchronously, through the graph, at the moment they are
needed.

The one contract that suggests otherwise is [[event|StockChange]] — declared in the order engine's protobuf, never
implemented on either side.
