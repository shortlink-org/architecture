## Overview

Produced by [[service|OMSService]] carrying [[event|OrderCompleted]] — and consumed by
[[service|OMSService]] as well.

The leaderboard consumer subscribes here and folds each completed order into
[[container|oms-redis-leaderboard]]. The service listening to its own event is deliberate: it keeps the ranking a
projection that can be rebuilt from the log, rather than a query over the order table.
See [[adr|adr-shop-0006-goods-leaderboard-read-model]].

Redelivery is handled in the consumer, not by the broker — the Lua script is keyed on order id plus
`aggregate_version`.
