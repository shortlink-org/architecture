## Overview

Produced by [[service|OMSService]] carrying [[event|OrderCreated]] — the moment a [[entity|Cart]] becomes an
[[entity|Order]].

No consumer inside the boundary. It is the event most likely to be wanted outside it: it carries the full item
list, customer and status, so a downstream reader does not need to call back.
