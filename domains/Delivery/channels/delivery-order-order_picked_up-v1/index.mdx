Produced by [[service|CourierEmulationService]] carrying [[event|OrderPickedUp]], consumed by
[[service|DeliveryService]], which moves the [[entity|Package]] to `IN_TRANSIT` and emits
[[event|PackageInTransit]].

Wired through `KAFKA_PICKUP_TOPIC`.
