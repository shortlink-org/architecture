## Overview

Produced by [[service|DeliveryService]] carrying [[event|CourierLocationUpdated]]. No consumer today.

## Not the same as the inbound location topic

Two location topics exist and they point in opposite directions:

| Topic | Direction | Producer |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| [[channel\|delivery.courier.location_received.v1]] | **in** | [[service\|CourierEmulationService]] |
| `delivery.courier.location.v1` | **out** | [[service\|DeliveryService]] |

The service consumes raw positions on the first and republishes them as domain events on this one.

## Configuration

| | |
|---|---|
| Partitions | 3 |
| Key | `courier_id` |
| Retention | **1 day** — high volume |

The shorter retention is the reason this event is not on [[channel|delivery.courier.events.v1]]: a week of
position updates has a very different storage profile from a week of registrations.
