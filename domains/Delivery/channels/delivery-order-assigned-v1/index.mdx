## Overview

Produced by [[service|DeliveryService]], consumed by [[service|CourierEmulationService]].

Carries the same fact as [[event|PackageAssigned]] on [[channel|delivery.package.status.v1]], but as **JSON**
rather than protobuf. The assignment is published to both topics in one call.

## Why it exists

Compatibility with the emulator, which reads JSON. It is not a second source of truth — if the two ever disagree,
the protobuf event on the status topic is authoritative.
