## Overview

Produced by [[service|CourierEmulationService]] carrying [[event|CourierLocationReceived]], consumed by
[[service|DeliveryService]].

The consumer writes the position to the TTL'd cache in [[container|delivery-redis]], appends to
`courier_location_history` in [[container|delivery-postgres]], and republishes it as
[[event|CourierLocationUpdated]] on [[channel|delivery.courier.location.v1]].

## The topic name is only correct in the deployment

> [!WARNING]
> The Rust constant `TOPIC_COURIER_LOCATION_UPDATES` defaults to `courier.location.updates` — a topic that is not
> declared anywhere. The correct name comes from `KAFKA_LOCATION_TOPIC` in
> `delivery/ops/delivery/values.yaml`. Read the Helm values, not the constant.
