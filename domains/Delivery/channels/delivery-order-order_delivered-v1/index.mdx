## Overview

Produced by [[service|CourierEmulationService]] carrying [[event|OrderDelivered]], consumed by
[[service|DeliveryService]].

Carries the outcome of the attempt, not only success — a failure lands the [[entity|Package]] in `NOT_DELIVERED`
with a structured reason and emits [[event|PackageNotDelivered]].

Wired through `KAFKA_DELIVERY_RESULT_TOPIC`.
