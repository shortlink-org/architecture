## Overview

Emitted when [[command|PickUpOrder]] succeeds — in practice, when the courier side confirms pickup on
[[channel|delivery.order.order_picked_up.v1]].

Carries the courier's location at pickup, and the `order_id` explicitly "for consumer lookup" — the payload is
shaped so [[service|OMSService]] can find the order without holding a package-to-order map.
