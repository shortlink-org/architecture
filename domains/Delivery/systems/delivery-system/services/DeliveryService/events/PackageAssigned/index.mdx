## Overview

Emitted by [[command|AssignOrder]], whether the courier was chosen manually or by automatic dispatch.

Unusually, it carries the pickup and delivery addresses, the delivery period and the customer phone — everything a
courier needs to act, so the courier side does not have to call back for details.

## Published twice, on purpose

| Topic | Payload | For |
|-------|---------|-----|
| [[channel\|delivery.package.status.v1]] | Protobuf | [[service\|OMSService]] — authoritative |
| [[channel\|delivery.order.assigned.v1]] | JSON | [[service\|CourierEmulationService]] — compatibility |

Both are written in the same publish call. If they ever disagree, the protobuf copy on the status topic is the
source of truth.
