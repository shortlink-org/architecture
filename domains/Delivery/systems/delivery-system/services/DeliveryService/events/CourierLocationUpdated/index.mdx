## Overview

Emitted after [[service|DeliveryService]] consumes a raw position off
[[channel|delivery.courier.location_received.v1]], writes it to the TTL'd cache in [[container|delivery-redis]] and
appends it to `courier_location_history`.

Carries only the courier id, the location and timestamps — the smallest payload in the domain, because it is also
by far the most frequent.

## Two location topics, opposite directions

| Topic | Direction |
|-------|-----------|
| [[channel\|delivery.courier.location_received.v1]] | inbound, from the courier side |
| [[channel\|delivery.courier.location.v1]] | outbound, this event |

Confusing the two is easy and the names do not help.
