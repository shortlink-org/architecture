## Overview

Emitted when [[command|AcceptOrder]] succeeds and a [[entity|Package]] enters the pool. Carries package id, order
id, customer id, status and both `created_at` and `occurred_at`.

This is the point where the [[domain|Delivery]] boundary takes ownership. The `package_id` it mints is returned
synchronously to [[service|OMSService]] as the gRPC response, and also travels on this event.

> events share the consolidated status topic and are told apart by an `event_type` header.
