## Overview

`DeliveryService.ArchiveCourier`. Sets [[entity|Courier]] status to `ARCHIVED`.

## Soft delete on purpose

The aggregate stays so that historical [[entity|Package]] records still resolve their courier. Archiving only stops
the courier being dispatchable.

## Effect

Emits [[event|CourierStatusChanged]].
