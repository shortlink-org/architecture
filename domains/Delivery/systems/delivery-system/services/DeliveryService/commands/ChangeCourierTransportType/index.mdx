## Overview

`DeliveryService.ChangeTransportType`.

## It recalculates max_load

Capacity is derived from transport, so this command recomputes `max_load` rather than expecting the caller to keep
the two consistent. Changing transport therefore changes how much a [[entity|Courier]] can carry, and can make an
already-loaded courier ineligible for further assignments.
