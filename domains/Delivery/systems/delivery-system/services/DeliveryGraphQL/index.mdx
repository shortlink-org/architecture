## Overview

Registered in [[service|ShopBFF]] as the `delivery` subgraph on port 4013, translating federated GraphQL into
[[service|DeliveryService]] gRPC.

```text
admin-ui / ui → BFF (Cosmo Router) → delivery-graphql (Go/Connect) → Delivery service (gRPC)
```

## The one that crosses a boundary

Every other subgraph adapts a service in its own domain. This one lives in [[domain|Delivery]] and is composed into
a graph owned by [[domain|Shop]] — the single synchronous path between the two boundaries.

It is worth being deliberate about: a customer viewing an order gets tracking in the same response, and the cost is
that the Shop graph will not compose without a Delivery-domain service being available at compose time.

## No state of its own

No database, no cache, no events. Tracking data belongs to [[service|DeliveryService]].
