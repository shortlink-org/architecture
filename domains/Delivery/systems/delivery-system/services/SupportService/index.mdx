## Overview

> [!WARNING]
> There is almost nothing here. `src/` contains two files — `index.php` and `config/init.php`. The tooling around
> it (Composer, Behat, Rector) is more developed than the service itself.

## Architecture diagram

## Intent

Customer service for the delivery process — enquiries and problems arising from a shipment. The boundary's
GLOSSARY defines Support that way, which is why it is documented in [[domain|Delivery]] rather than in
[[domain|Shop]].

## Stack

PHP with OPCache, Composer for dependencies, [Behat](https://docs.behat.org/) for BDD scenarios and Rector for
automated refactoring. Two decisions are recorded: [[adr|adr-support-0001-init]] and
[[adr|adr-support-0002-network]].

> [!NOTE]
> The repository's root README links this service at `./boundaries/delivery/support/README.md`. That path does not
> exist — the service is at the repository root as `support/`. The same table lists a `geolocation` service with a
> matching broken link and no directory at all.
