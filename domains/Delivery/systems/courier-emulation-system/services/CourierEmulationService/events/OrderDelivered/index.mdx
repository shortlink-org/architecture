## Overview

Published by [[service|CourierEmulationService]], consumed by [[service|DeliveryService]].

Despite the name it carries the **outcome**, not only success. A failed attempt lands the [[entity|Package]] in
`NOT_DELIVERED` with a structured reason and produces [[event|PackageNotDelivered]]; a success produces
[[event|PackageDelivered]].
