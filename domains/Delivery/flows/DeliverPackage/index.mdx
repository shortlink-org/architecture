## Where it starts

The `AcceptOrder` call at the top is the last step of [[flow|Checkout]] — a synchronous gRPC call from a Temporal
activity, not an event.

## The return path is the interesting half

Delivery does not update the order. It publishes to [[channel|delivery.package.status.v1]] and
[[service|OMSService]] restates what it hears as the order's *own* events, so nothing outside this boundary has to
subscribe to a delivery topic.

Because Kafka delivers at least once and out of order, the consumer records each message id in
`oms.delivery_inbox_messages` and drops updates that are stale for the order's current state before applying them.

## What the flow does not cover

[[event|PackageRequiresHandling]] — a package that cannot proceed and needs a human. Nothing consumes it, and the
query that would surface such packages, [[query|GetPackagePool]], is not exposed over gRPC.
