## Who calls these is not recorded

The four commands are RPCs on [[service|DeliveryService]]. No admin surface for them appears anywhere in this
catalog — [[service|DeliveryGraphQL]] exposes tracking only, and the Shop back office
([[service|AdminUI]]) is the catalogue's admin, not the fleet's. The operator above is the use case, not a
documented application.

## Capacity is a consequence of the vehicle

`max_load` is derived from `transport_type` on registration, and re-derived by
[[command|ChangeCourierTransportType]] later. There is no way to hand a courier a capacity directly.

## Archiving keeps the aggregate

`ARCHIVED` removes a courier from dispatch and leaves the record in place, so a [[entity|Package]] delivered last
month still resolves the courier who carried it.

## Status changes twice over

This flow covers the deliberate transitions. The other producer of `CourierStatusChanged` is dispatch itself — see
[[flow|DeliverPackage]] — which changes a courier's load without anyone issuing a command.
