## Keyed by order, not by package

Both queries take an OMS order id. The customer holds an [[entity|Order]] and has never seen a [[entity|Package]],
so the cross-boundary reference the package carries exists for exactly this read.

## Why Redis is not a cache here

A transition happens in whichever replica handled it; the stream is held open by whichever replica the customer
reached. These are rarely the same pod. `infrastructure/messaging/tracking_pubsub.rs` bridges them over
[[container|delivery-redis]] — without it, live tracking would only work by coincidence.

## The cost of composing across the boundary

[[service|DeliveryGraphQL]] is the single synchronous path from [[domain|Shop]] into [[domain|Delivery]]. It buys a
customer their tracking in the same response as their order, and it means the Shop graph will not compose unless a
Delivery-domain service is available at compose time.
